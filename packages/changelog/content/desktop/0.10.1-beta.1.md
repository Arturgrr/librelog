## License

- Librelog is now licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later** (AGPL-3.0-or-later). Earlier releases stay available under the MIT license, and the parts that came from Anarlog remain under MIT too (see `LICENSE-MIT`).
- Contributors now sign a short Contributor License Agreement on their first pull request. See `CLA.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Dictation

- If the microphone fails in the middle of a dictation, Librelog no longer throws the dictation away. It stops, transcribes and inserts what was recorded, and tells you the microphone stopped. Only a dictation with almost nothing recorded still fails.
- A dictation whose microphone simply stopped no longer stays in "recording" until you press the shortcut.

## KDE Plasma and Wayland

- **Dictation into Konsole and Yakuake works**: Librelog asks KWin which window is in front, so terminals get Control + Shift + V and Qt apps a normal paste, even with KDE's accessibility off. The dictation history names the app, and **Send after pasting** works on KDE.
- **Copying a screenshot or recording** goes through Wayland's clipboard first, so it no longer silently keeps the old clipboard. Copying a GIF or a recording into Dolphin copies the file instead of moving it.
- KDE's remote-control permission for pasting no longer keeps the "controlled remotely" indicator on the whole time when KDE can't remember the approval, and pasting works with keyboard layouts that have no V.
- Librelog uses one app id however it starts, including at login and from a terminal, so KDE no longer asks twice for its shortcuts and permissions. Shortcut and permission dialogs open above Librelog, and the capture area selector gets the focus when opened by a shortcut.
- **Shortcuts on Linux**: a double tap locks the dictation shortcut, as on macOS. Alt + Shift + 4, 5 and 6 work on KDE. Changing a shortcut no longer leaves old entries in KDE's Shortcuts settings. Annotate's pen lasts as long as you hold its shortcut when the desktop reports the release.
- The tray icon is dark on a light Plasma panel and white on a dark one, and notifications follow KDE's colour scheme.
- Deleting a capture moves it to the Trash without GNOME's `gio`.
- The Linux guide has a KDE Plasma section.

## Known limitations

- None of the KDE changes have been tried on a real Plasma desktop yet. Please report what breaks, with your distribution and Plasma version.
- On KDE, Annotate's summary has no app name, and password fields in Qt apps are only recognised with Qt accessibility turned on (see the Linux guide).
- Librelog's windows still run through XWayland on Wayland, so with fractional scaling they can look blurry or small.
- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch on a Mac needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- On a Mac, Librelog needs Apple silicon (M1 or later).
