## What's new in your language

- Release notes now appear in the app's language, starting with Portuguese, and fall back to English when a version has no translation. GitHub releases also carry the Portuguese notes.

## Dictation

- **Your Dictionary reaches local Whisper and Qwen3-ASR models**: the words in your Dictionary, your replacements and what dictation learned are given to the model as a glossary, most important first, for dictation and meetings. If the model repeats the glossary or makes it up in silence, that text is removed. Parakeet and other models that can't take a glossary are unchanged.
- **AI cleanup can be tuned** in **Settings → Dictation**: choose a faster model just for cleanup, how long to wait for it (3, 6, 10 or 20 seconds) before your words are typed as you said them, and your own instructions, such as "Write in British English". **Try it** shows what dictation would type for a sentence you write.
- **Copy recent dictation**, in the menu bar or tray, lists your five latest dictations to copy with a click. With dictation history off, it stays empty.

## Onboarding

- A new **Try dictation** step shows your shortcut, turns dictation on, asks for the permissions it needs and lets you dictate into a field right there.
- **Continue without a model** lets you finish onboarding without choosing a transcription model; meetings and dictation ask for one the first time you use them.

## Annotate

- The narration uses your built-in or wired microphone when the default is a Bluetooth headset, like dictation.
- If the microphone stops in the middle of an annotation, the narration recorded so far is kept, Librelog keeps trying to reopen it, and the audio stays in step with the screen. You're told when it stops and when it's back.
- The capsule shows the microphone's level while you record.
- Without microphone permission, an annotation now records the screen without narration instead of refusing to start.

## Updates

- An update download that was interrupted continues where it stopped when you open Librelog again, and it's only installed after its signature checks out. This applies to the Mac app and the AppImage; RPM and deb installs still update through your package manager.

## Known limitations

- These changes were written without a Mac, a Linux desktop or a Windows computer to try them on. Automated checks build and test them, but nobody has used them yet.
- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch on a Mac needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- On a Mac, Librelog needs Apple silicon (M1 or later).
