## Dictation

- Dictation no longer cuts off your last word. After you let go of the shortcut, it keeps listening for a moment, a little longer while you are still speaking.
- **Keep microphone ready**, in **Settings → Dictation**, keeps the microphone open while dictation is on, so each dictation also catches the moment before you press the shortcut. Your system's microphone indicator stays on the whole time, and no audio is kept unless you dictate. It is off until you turn it on.
- Dictations no longer stop after 5 minutes. Long recordings, in dictation and in meetings, are now split for transcription at a quiet moment instead of in the middle of a word.
- **Spoken commands**, without AI and on your computer, in Portuguese and English, each with its own switch:
  - **Remove hesitations** drops sounds like "éé", "hã", "um" and "uh". Real words, such as "é" in "ele é bom", are kept.
  - **Spoken formatting**: say "nova linha", "novo parágrafo", "abre aspas" … "fecha aspas" or "abre parênteses" … "fecha parênteses" (or "new line", "open quote" and so on).
  - **Spoken symbols**: say "arroba" for @, "hashtag" for #, "underline" for _, and "ponto com" or "ponto br" for .com and .br. "joao arroba acme ponto com" becomes "joao@acme.com".
- **Presets** pair a spoken language with a speech model, like Portuguese with one model and English with another. A shortcut you choose switches between them, and the tray menu lists them. A switch during a dictation or a meeting waits for it to end, and a local model starts loading right away. The preset changes the speech model meetings use too.
- **Send after pasting** presses Return after your dictation is inserted, so chats and coding agents send it, in every app or only in the apps you list. It only sends when the whole dictation went into the field that still has focus, and never in password fields, after editing a selection by voice, or in Librelog itself.
- On a Mac or Windows PC, when your default microphone is a Bluetooth headset, dictation uses the built-in or wired microphone instead, so music in the headset doesn't drop to call quality. Settings shows which microphone is used. Turn it off with **Avoid Bluetooth headset microphone**; a microphone you choose is always used.
- **Settings → Dictation** shows your microphone's input level live, so you can check it hears you.
- Dictations of any practical length can be inserted.

## Known limitations

- These changes were written without a Mac, a Linux desktop or a Windows computer to try them on. Automated checks build and test them, but nobody has dictated with them yet.
- Spoken commands are off by default because everyday speech can collide with them, such as "arroba" as a unit of weight.
- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- On a Mac, Librelog needs Apple silicon (M1 or later).

Parts of this release follow [Hex](https://github.com/anomalyco/hex), an MIT-licensed dictation app.
