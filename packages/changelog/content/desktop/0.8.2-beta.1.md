## Annotate

- When you finish an annotation, its capsule stays on screen and shows that the annotation is being prepared, the way the dictation pill does while it transcribes. A check mark tells you when the image and the summary are ready to paste, and a red mark when something went wrong.
- **Option + Shift + A** pressed while an annotation is still being prepared waits for it and pastes it, instead of the one before. If it takes longer than 30 seconds, Librelog tells you so and pastes nothing.
- A new **Pen shortcut**, **Shift + C** by default, turns drawing on and off while you record. Librelog holds it only while an annotation is recording, so the rest of the time it types a capital C as usual. Change it in **Settings → Annotate**.
- While you draw, the mouse wheel and the trackpad scroll the app under the pointer, as they do without the pen. This needs the **Accessibility** permission that dictation already asks for; without it the pen keeps the wheel, as before.
- The capsule now looks like the dictation pill: the same size, colours and motion, in light and dark mode.

## Dictation

- The waveform in the dictation pill could stop moving for a while as you spoke, then start again on its own, even though everything you said was recorded. This happened when a local transcription model was busy on the same Mac's graphics chip. The pill now keeps moving.
- If the pill still freezes, open **Console** and look for messages that start with "Dictation HUD", and tell us what they say.

## Linux

- The Annotate capsule has the dictation pill's look and the same preparing, ready and failed states.
- The pen shortcut works on X11. On Wayland your desktop would keep its keys for good, so there is no pen shortcut there: use the capsule's pen.
- The wheel scrolls the app under the pen, one click at a time. Under XWayland it reaches only apps that also run through XWayland.

## Known limitations

- These changes were written without a Mac to try them on. Automated checks build and test them on macOS, but nobody has annotated or dictated with them yet.
- The first click right after a scroll under the pen reaches the app underneath instead of starting a stroke. The next one draws.
- Librelog is not notarized by Apple yet. The first launch needs **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- On a Mac, Librelog needs Apple silicon (M1 or later).
